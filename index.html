Only Human

Only Human is a simple table top role-playing game for telling sci-fi stories about competent people investigating dangerous situations on the fringes of an optimistic future.

Only Human 0.4.0

2026-09-27

  • I'm now really trying to conjure The Martian or Project Hail Mary.
  • Human-vs-Environment stores can have a lot of tension and challenge in them without ever needing to have any of that hinge on causing harm to another person.
  • This is a complete revision of the system.
  • 16 A5 pages containing all the rules for the core of the game.
  • Still missing an adventure/scenario or any concrete guidance on how to make one.
  • A very clear procedure to help avoid rolling where unnecessary.
  • if you do roll you roll a d8, add a stat (0 to 3) and add 1 if you have a relevant skill.
  • If you beat a public-before-you-confirmed-you-were-rolling difficulty number you do what you set out to do.
  • If you beat it by four or more something extra gets better.
  • If you miss the difficulty (but not by more than four), either you succeed at a cost or fail but get some lesser benefit.
  • If you fail by more than four things go very badly (but you gain one XP).
  • XP is a spendable resource, movement is broken into zones, there's no HP, you heal by treating the specific injuries you have taken, and a bunch of other stuff, but you should just read the rules, they are better written than this.
